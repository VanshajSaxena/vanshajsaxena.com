I’m Vanshaj Saxena, a **software engineer at MyPay**. I work across backend, frontend, and UI/UX, with a focus on understanding the whole problem before choosing the tools.

## What I’m working on

At MyPay, I’m working on a visual website editor, designed and created an employee management system, and am helping rewrite a roughly **200,000-line legacy frontend** using AI-assisted development and a modern stack. The rewrite is ongoing.

## How I got here

[Auction Hub](/projects/auction-system/) deepened my work with Java, Spring Boot, API contracts, and security. During an Infosys internship in Mysuru, I worked on [Fable](/projects/fable/) as a backend architect and developer, and served as Scrum Master in a ten-person team.

In [CareNote](/projects/carenote/), I built the OCR-enabled medical document scanner and maintained the team repository by merging others’ pull requests.

[Printit](/projects/printit/) took me from React interfaces and NestJS services to Electron state machines and Windows printing APIs. It is now dormant and unmaintained, and never acquired users. I keep its case studies here because the architecture and implementation are useful examples of how I approach complex engineering problems.

## How I work

Solve the right problem. Make the boundaries clear. Improve reliability iteratively.

I like working across layers: understanding what someone needs to do, then making the interfaces and systems support that workflow. I also write about the trade-offs along the way, including [state machines](/posts/how-i-architected-and-developed-a-pure-state-machine-inside-the-renderer-process-of-an-electron-application/) and [reproducible Nix environments](/posts/how-i-use-nix-and-nixos-to-maintain-a-purely-functional-development-environment/).
